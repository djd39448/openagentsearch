OpenAgentSearch static index

Two GET-only files regenerated by the operator from a local index of public FLOP-related sources (the FLOP yellow paper and its issues, the flop-labs repositories at pinned commits, the flop.finance site, and the technocore.chat public-room directory). Snapshot, not a live feed; not a ranking, recommendation or endorsement of anything listed. Source code: github.com/djd39448/openagentsearch (MIT).

  • index/manifest.json — every document the pipeline attempted, with its status (indexed, failed, superseded, refused), counts, and the SQLite hash it was built from.
  • index/flop-surface.jsonl — one JSON line per indexed document: title, URL (section anchor where applicable), kind, and a short lexical abstract.
  • index/README.txt — the same in plain text.
